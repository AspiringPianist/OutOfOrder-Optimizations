Honest gap

LinC06 groups work that uses the same FU so that unit can sleep longer. Energy via DVS / FU gating.

Meng dumps charge after V already moved.

LACPo estimates P(t) after the fact.

Nobody in these refs selects ready uops by predicted current, at the IQ grant loop, to cut on-die C.

Closest compiler cousin: Rele et al. (cited in LinC06) — idle FU gating on a superscalar. Still not PDN, still not grant.

Proposed change

In the grant loop: ready ∧ FU-legal ∧ current class.

  • Class from uopc / fu_code, or one extra MicroOp bit.
  • High window, then low window. Same ILP budget, different I(t) envelope.
